Nilesh Vaghela's journey in the technology industry began in 1998, where he started his career with a focus on open-source infrastructure and automation. In 2014, he embarked on a new path, diving into the world of AWS and the AWS community.

As an experienced cloud architect and DevOps engineer, Nilesh has been actively involved in the AWS ecosystem, serving over 200 customers with his expertise in architecting, designing, deploying, troubleshooting, migrating, and optimizing cloud workloads on AWS.

Nilesh's commitment to the AWS community is truly remarkable. He has hosted more than 10 AWS Community Day events in Ahmedabad and Vadodara, Gujarat, India, sharing his knowledge and insights with fellow enthusiasts. Additionally, Nilesh has delivered numerous sessions and workshops at various community events, universities, colleges, and other communities, covering both open-source and cloud-related topics.

Recognizing his exceptional contributions, Nilesh has been honored as an AWS Community Hero, a prestigious title that acknowledges his dedication to empowering and inspiring others in the AWS community.
